Privacy policy

Last updated 29 October 2026 (follow check added)

This covers the automatic reply on @justdroppedtech's Instagram reels. When you comment a reel's keyword, we send you the guide link in a direct message.

What we collect

  • Your Instagram account ID and username
  • The comment you left, its ID, and the reel it was on
  • When we sent you the message
  • Whether you follow @justdroppedtech, checked once you tap Get the guide or reply to us

Why

Only to send you the guide you asked for, to check that you follow us before sending it, and to make sure you do not get it twice. We also count how many people asked for each guide.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell or share your data with anyone.
  • We do not use it for ads.
  • We do not message you again unless you reply to us.

How long we keep it

90 days, then it is deleted.

Where it is stored

On Google Cloud, in the account that runs the reply. Messages are sent through Meta's official Instagram API.
